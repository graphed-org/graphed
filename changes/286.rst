.. under: 0.0.12 (unreleased)

* Building a record one field at a time (``rec[name] = value``, ``gak.with_field``) records in time
  close to linear in the number of fields, and column projection replays it the same way: on a
  typetracer, awkward's ``with_field`` now runs over only the fields the call changes, and the
  others are kept as they were. Forms, plans and projections are unchanged. Adding 2000 fields to
  a jagged selection records in 1.1 s instead of 13.7 s (#286).
