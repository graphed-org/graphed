.. under: 0.0.12 (unreleased)

* Running a graph that builds a record one field at a time no longer re-broadcasts the whole
  record for each new field: on real arrays, as on a typetracer (#286), awkward's ``with_field``
  runs over only the fields the call changes and the others are kept as they were, wherever
  awkward's call leaves the layers above the record as they were; elsewhere awkward's own call
  runs. Values and types are awkward's. Adding 2000 fields to a jagged selection runs in 2.0 s
  instead of 14.8 s, and to an option-typed one in 1.4 s instead of 37.2 s (#304).
