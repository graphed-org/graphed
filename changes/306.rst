.. under: 0.0.12 (unreleased)

* ``graphed.head``, ``graphed.materialize(entries=)`` and ``graphed.debug.trace`` hand a
  ``partition=True`` function the partition they read: the first partition, cut to the entries
  read. They used to give it none, so anything downstream of such a function raised. First
  entries that span several partitions are refused, as they are for an op that pairs its operands
  chunk by chunk (#306).
