* A backend declares the ops whose inputs may hold different rows of one chunk with
  ``rows_unaligned(op, params)``, replacing ``AwkwardBackend.unaligned_ops``, so it can exempt one
  behavior method (a coffea cross-reference's ``_apply_global_index``) rather than every ``method``
  op. ``graphed.materialize`` evaluates an op whose operands pair by chunk (``pairs_by_chunk``)
  partition by partition instead of once over the joined chunks, where it silently took the wrong
  rows past the first partition. An ``entries=`` or ``head()`` sample spanning partitions refuses
  such an op, and so does ``materialize`` when it sits downstream of a reduction, exchange or join,
  where it returned a wrong whole-dataset value (#233).
