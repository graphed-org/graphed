* ``str()``, ``print()``, ``"%s" %`` and f-strings or ``format()`` without a spec describe a
  deferred value (an array, a whole-array reduction such as ``gak.sum(x)``, a single record such
  as ``events[0]``, or a ``Varied`` of them) as its ``repr()`` does. A format spec
  (``f"{x:.3f}"``, ``format(x, "d")``) asks for the calculated value and raises
  ``DeferredTypeError`` at the user's line, as ``float()`` and ``"%f" %`` do; on an array it used
  to raise a plain ``TypeError`` (#219, #313).
